## Product Lab

AgiWish is my AI product lab.

Every repository here is an experiment in turning raw intent into a working product:

```text
Human intent -> Product judgment -> Prompt system -> AI execution -> Prototype -> Feedback
```

I use AI as the production layer. My job is to define the problem, shape the workflow, control the product experience, and decide what is worth building.

## Operating Principles

- AI should not just answer. It should move the workflow forward.
- Prompts are not text tricks. They are product interfaces.
- Agents need clear jobs, boundaries, memory, and feedback loops.
- Fast prototypes are useful only when they test real product assumptions.
- The future PM does not only write PRDs. The future PM designs systems that write, run, and improve products.
